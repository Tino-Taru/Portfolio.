EcoTrack

An environmental data platform that turns site data into cost-saving actions and evidence for mid-size mining and industrial operators. Built by Team Artemi9, nine founders from the University of Johannesburg.

Open the live platform

My role

I served as Lead Researcher and Literature Review Lead, and I also worked on the progressive web app (PWA), the installable version of the platform.

Problem statement

Mid-size mining and industrial operators keep environmental data in scattered spreadsheets and pay consultants for every report. They have no single view of their obligations, what acting on them would cost, or the evidence regulators and funders expect. EcoTrack was built to close that gap.

The idea

The project began with a Mpumalanga climate focus. We have since pivoted to a broader business: operators keep environmental data in spreadsheets and pay consultants for every report, so EcoTrack puts it in one place.

  • See it: site data, documents, obligations and deadlines, with an AI assistant
  • Decide on it: ranked recommendations with cost, annual saving and payback
  • Prove it: reports and an evidence trail for regulators and funders

Two pilots are onboarding: Makunyula Anthracite Mine and the GPI Mjejane cassava project. There are no paying customers yet.

ResKitchen

A campus meal prep and catering venture for University of Johannesburg residence students: affordable pre-ordered meals collected at set times, plus event catering for societies and house committees. Developed for Entrepreneurship 3A as a lean canvas, pricing model and working website prototype.

Open the live platform

My role

I served as Logistics Manager, covering order fulfilment, labelling, collection-point setup and event catering coordination.

Problem statement

Students in residence often cannot cook daily, so they rely on delivery apps and fast food that strain tight budgets and nutrition.

The idea

  • Pre-order: a weekly menu and online order form with fixed collection slots
  • Collect: meals picked up at the residence foyer, which keeps delivery costs out
  • Cater: per-head packages for society and house committee events

This is a concept prototype: orders are not processed.
